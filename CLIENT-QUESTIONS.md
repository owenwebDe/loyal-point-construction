# Loyal Point Construction: a few questions for your website

Thank you! These answers go straight onto your website, so please only tell us what is
true. Short answers are fine. Anything you skip, we simply leave off the site.

Already answered: open 24 hours. (Is that every day, including Sunday?)

## Must have

1. **Your story.** What year did Loyal Point start, and who started it?
2. **Owner or contact name.** Who should customers ask for when they call?
3. **Where you work.** Only Lagos, or do you also take jobs in other states?
4. **Site visits.** Do you come to the customer's place to measure? Is it free?
5. **Installation.** Do you install the doors, windows and gates yourselves?
6. **Your services.** For each one, one or two lines about what you make or do:
   - Iron burglary proof
   - Safety doors
   - Windows (which designs?)
   - Containers (do you make them, modify them, or both?)
   - General repairs
   - Iron bending
   - Pillars and columns
   - Beams
   - Decking

## Nice to have

7. **Team.** How many people work with you? Can we show a photo of the team?
8. **Past jobs.** Any projects you are proud of? Area (e.g. "Magodo"), what you built,
   and a photo. Only ones the customer is happy to show.
9. **Customer words.** Any real messages from happy customers we may quote, with
   their first name?
10. **Why you.** In your own words, why should someone choose Loyal Point?

## Photos: what to send

The door photos you sent are a great start. To make every service look as good:

- **Every service, not only doors:** burglary proof, windows, containers, pillars,
  beams, decking, bending and repair jobs. Three to six photos each is ideal.
- **Straight on:** stand in front of the item, phone at the middle of its height, step
  back until the whole thing fits with a little space around it.
- **Cloudy daylight or shade is perfect** (like your door photos). Avoid strong sun and
  avoid flash.
- **Close-ups:** handle, lock, hinges, the pattern, a clean weld.
- **Finished jobs at the customer's place:** a door fitted in its wall, a gate on its
  pillars, burglary proof on a window.
- **The workshop and the team at work.** Real people working is the best photo of all.
- **Send originals:** on WhatsApp, choose "Document" instead of "Gallery" so they stay
  sharp. The photos so far came through compressed.
- **Short videos are very welcome:** 10–20 seconds of welding, cutting or installing,
  phone held still.
